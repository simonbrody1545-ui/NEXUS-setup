The public NEXUS website is a static information and download site. Your private NEXUS dashboard is a separate service running on your own computer.

What visitors can see

Pages about NEXUS, its features, setup guidance and any installer you choose to publish.

LOCAL DASHBOARD

What stays on your PC

Your local dashboard is not automatically published by hosting this website. Keep it bound to localhost unless you have deliberately secured remote access.

IMPORTANT

Protect private files

Never upload API keys, passwords, private certificates, personal configuration files or logs containing secrets to a public repository or website.
